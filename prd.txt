Document Overview

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) defines the MVP scope, user experience, functional requirements,
operational needs and launch considerations for Apex Finance. It consolidates the product vision and requirements
supplied for the project, with Nigeria-focused implementation notes.
Important: This is a product-planning document, not legal advice or evidence that Apex Finance holds any
financial-services licence. The operating model, regulated partners, product disclosures and regulatory approvals must
be confirmed with qualified Nigerian legal and compliance advisers before launch.

1. Product Vision

A simple, secure mobile app that helps users save money, access funds easily and invest in company shares--all
from one platform.
Core values: Simplicity · Trust · Accessibility · Growth

1.1 Product Summary

Apex Finance is a mobile-first financial platform designed to bring everyday savings, wallet funding, withdrawals,
transfers and beginner-friendly share investing into one coherent experience. The MVP is Nigeria-first, with
architecture and operating processes designed to support future African-market expansion.

1.2 Product Principles

· Simple: Use plain language, predictable flows and clear next steps.
· Transparent: Disclose fees, rates, settlement timing, restrictions and investment risks before users commit.
· Secure: Protect accounts, transactions and personal information through layered controls.
· Inclusive: Support different income patterns and users who are new to saving or investing.
· Responsible: Offer regulated financial and investment services only through appropriately authorised entities and
partners.

1.3 Problem Statement

Many users struggle with fragmented financial services--separate apps for savings, withdrawals and
investments--leading to confusion, low trust and poor financial growth. Existing platforms can be complex, unfriendly
to beginners or inaccessible to underserved users.

1.4 Solution

A unified mobile app that combines digital savings, seamless withdrawals and beginner-friendly share investing,
designed with simplicity, transparency and security at its core.

2. Goals & Objectives

2.2 User Goals

3. Target Users & Personas

Persona 1: Salary Earner

· Needs structured savings around a regular pay cycle.
· May withdraw or transfer funds monthly.
· Is a beginner or cautious investor and values clear explanations.

Persona 2: Freelancer / SME Owner

· Receives irregular income.
· Needs flexible deposits and visibility over cash flow.
· Wants accessible ways to save and potentially grow surplus funds.

Persona 3: First-Time Investor

· May start with a relatively small amount of capital.
· Needs understandable company and market information.
· Needs risk explanations and clear order confirmations; investments must not be presented as risk-free.

6. Functional Requirements

6.1 User Onboarding & Authentication

Requirements
· Sign up using a Nigerian phone number and/or email address.
· Verify contact details using a one-time password (OTP).
· Create a secure transaction PIN.
· Offer biometric login where supported by the device.
· Provide password/PIN reset and account-recovery flows.
Acceptance criteria
· A typical user can complete basic account creation in under two minutes, excluding external verification delays.
· OTP expires after a configurable period and can be resent subject to rate limits.
· Sensitive actions require appropriate step-up authentication.

6.2 KYC Verification

Proposed verification levels
· Level 1 -- Basic: Basic personal details and contact verification, with restricted transaction limits and feature access
as permitted by the partner and applicable rules.
· Level 2 -- Verified: Required identity checks, which may include NIN/BVN validation, an accepted identity document
and selfie/liveness verification, depending on the service and partner requirements.
Requirements
· Collect only information necessary for the relevant service and legal purpose.
· Support identity-document capture or approved identity-provider verification.
· Show clear KYC states: Not started, In review, Verified, Action required or Failed.

· Explain what is missing when additional information is required.
· Apply transaction limits and access controls according to approved KYC policy.
Nigeria implementation note: BVN and NIN are sensitive identifiers. Use approved verification channels, explicit
notices, strict access controls and data-minimisation practices. The final KYC tiers and limits must be set with the
licensed bank, payment and investment partners and compliance counsel.

6.3 Wallet

· Display available wallet balance and, where applicable, balances that are pending or restricted.
· Allow wallet funding through supported bank transfer and/or card channels.
· Show transaction history with date, amount, status, reference and transaction type.
· Provide receipts and clear pending, successful, reversed and failed states.
· Do not imply wallet funds are bank deposits or deposit-insured unless the actual legal structure supports that claim.

6.4 Savings Module

The Save area supports three savings products. Product rates, minimums, maximums, fees, access restrictions and
partner arrangements must be configured and disclosed before a user creates a plan.

Flexible Savings

· Create a named savings plan and deposit an initial amount.
· Allow additional deposits at any time, subject to product limits.
· Allow withdrawals according to the displayed product rules and available balance.
· Display the applicable variable rate, calculation method and interest-crediting frequency, if interest is offered.

Fixed Savings

· Create a plan using a one-time deposit and a selected lock period (illustrative options: 30, 60, 90 or 180 days).
· Display the rate, maturity date, projected payout and early-access rules before confirmation.
· Prevent edits to locked principal and tenure after activation, unless the product terms explicitly allow them.
· At maturity, make principal and applicable returns available according to the disclosed payout process.

Target Savings

· Create a goal with a name, target amount and target date.
· Support manual contributions and scheduled automatic contributions where the funding mandate is supported.
· Allow users to see progress, remaining amount and contribution history.
· Provide clear rules for pausing auto-save, missed contributions, early access and any interest or charges.
Acceptance criteria
· Balances and transaction records reconcile with the ledger and update promptly after confirmed transactions.
· Users see a plan summary and applicable terms before confirming.
· The app never describes a rate or return as guaranteed unless legally and contractually guaranteed.

6.5 Withdrawals & Transfers

· Withdraw eligible wallet funds to a verified, supported Nigerian bank account.
· Support wallet-to-wallet transfers only where the service is enabled and permitted.
· Confirm sensitive actions using PIN and/or biometrics, with additional verification when required.

· Show fees, limits and estimated processing time before confirmation.
Edge cases
· Insufficient available balance.
· Incorrect or unsupported beneficiary details.
· Bank or payment-network downtime.
· Pending, failed, reversed or delayed transfer.
· Duplicate submission or timeout after a transaction request.
Every transaction must have a unique reference and a clear status. A timeout must not cause a user to unknowingly
submit duplicate transfers.

6.6 Investment / Shares Module

· List eligible companies and instruments available through the licensed investment partner.
· Display price, currency, market status, timestamp or delay indicator, and relevant company information.
· Support buy and sell orders according to broker/exchange capabilities.
· Support fractional investing only if the broker, custodian, market structure and applicable rules permit it.
· Show order status, trade confirmation, fees, taxes where applicable and settlement information.
· Provide portfolio holdings, cost basis where available, current value and realised/unrealised performance where
supported.
· Provide risk disclosures and make clear that share prices can fall as well as rise.
Nigeria implementation note: NGX-listed securities and any foreign securities must be offered through appropriately
authorised capital-market operators and approved custody/settlement arrangements. Apex Finance must not imply
that it is itself a broker, exchange or investment adviser unless duly authorised.

7. Non-Functional Requirements

7.1 Performance

· Target app launch and primary-screen load time below three seconds under agreed test conditions.
· Refresh investment prices within the service level agreed with the market-data provider; show timestamps and
delays.
· Use pagination or incremental loading for long histories and company lists.

7.2 Security

· Encrypt data in transit and at rest using current, industry-accepted standards.
· Use role-based access control, least privilege, audit logs and secure secrets management.
· Apply multi-factor or step-up authentication to sensitive actions.
· Protect PINs and credentials using secure hashing and platform security controls; never store them in plaintext.
· Conduct vulnerability testing, security reviews and incident-response exercises.

7.3 Availability & Reliability

· Initial availability target: 99.5%, excluding agreed maintenance windows and clearly defined third-party
dependencies.
· Use monitoring, backups, disaster recovery and reconciliation processes.
· Define incident severity, escalation and customer communication procedures.

· Design mobile-first for common Android and iOS screen sizes.
· Use readable text, sufficient contrast, clear focus states and accessible labels.

8. UX & UI Requirements

· Mobile-first interface with persistent bottom navigation: Home, Save, Invest and Profile.
· Use clear, descriptive calls to action and consistent confirmation patterns.
· Show fees, rates, limits, risks and processing expectations before users commit.
· Provide helpful empty, loading, success, pending and error states.
· Use local currency formatting (s) for Nigeria and make currency explicit for investments.
· Avoid misleading urgency, guaranteed-growth claims or dark patterns.

9. Notifications

· Transaction alerts for deposits, withdrawals, transfers and reversals.
· Savings reminders and target-progress updates, where users have opted in.
· Investment order and trade confirmations.
· KYC status and action-required alerts.
· Security alerts for new devices, credential changes and suspicious activity.
· Allow users to manage non-essential notification preferences while retaining legally required or essential security
notices.

10. Admin & Back Office

· User and KYC case management with permission controls.
· Transaction monitoring, reconciliation and exception queues.
· Savings-product configuration, subject to maker-checker approval.
· Investment instrument/listing and market-data monitoring through partner integrations.
· Fraud and suspicious-activity monitoring with escalation workflows.
· Customer-support tools and complaint tracking.
· Operational, financial, compliance and product analytics with audit trails.

11. Compliance & Legal Considerations -- Nigeria First

The final regulatory model depends on which entity legally provides each service. Apex Finance should obtain
specialist advice and confirm the roles, licences and contractual responsibilities of all partners before launch.
· CBN: Confirm whether wallet, payment, deposit-taking or other activities are provided by a CBN-licensed bank or
payment service provider, and ensure Apex's own activities do not cross into regulated activity without authorisation.
· SEC Nigeria: Share dealing, investment intermediation, portfolio services, custody and investment advice must be
handled by appropriately registered or licensed capital-market operators as applicable.

· NDPC / Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: Establish a lawful basis for processing, privacy notices, data-subject
request handling, retention schedules, processor contracts, security controls and breach-response procedures.

· AML/CFT: Implement risk-based customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening and
suspicious-activity escalation as applicable to the regulated entities and their obligations.

· Consumer protection: Disclose fees, rates, product restrictions, complaint channels, transaction timelines and
material service changes clearly.

· Deposit protection: Do not use NDIC-protection claims or logos unless the relevant funds and partner arrangement
are confirmed to qualify and the wording is approved.

For expansion into other African countries, complete a separate country assessment covering local banking/payment
rules, securities regulation, privacy law, consumer protection, tax, FX and cross-border transfer requirements.

17. Key Disclaimers

This PRD describes intended product functionality and does not constitute an offer to accept deposits, provide
payment services, trade securities or provide investment advice. Actual products, rates, returns, fees, eligibility, limits,
settlement arrangements and legal protections must match approved contracts and applicable law.
Apex Finance should clearly identify the licensed institutions and capital-market operators responsible for each
regulated service. Do not claim that funds are insured, guaranteed, risk-free or instantly available unless the claim is
factually and legally supported.
