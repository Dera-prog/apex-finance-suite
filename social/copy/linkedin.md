## Post copy (paste as-is)

Most fintech case studies end with a polished hero shot and a KPI nobody measured.

This one ends with four working URLs.

Apex Finance is a savings, payments and share-investing product for Nigeria. I worked from a 10-page PRD, designed the two core flows in Figma, and built the front end: mobile app, desktop workspace, marketing site, and the case study itself.

Six weeks. One design system.

Three decisions I'd defend in review:

**1. Show the rate on the plan, not in a footnote.**
A fixed plan says 16% p.a., opened 20 October, matures 18 January, and states that the balance is locked until then. If a customer can commit against a number, that number belongs on the same screen as the commitment.

**2. Put the fee before the send button.**
The transfer sheet states the ₦0.00 fee and instant settlement before money moves, and every movement ends in a receipt with a reference. Trust is a layout problem before it's a copy problem.

**3. Label what isn't live.**
Prices are delayed 15 minutes — that's in the header, not buried. And the first thing the product says about itself is that it's a portfolio concept, not a licensed institution.

What I didn't do: invent results. The KPIs are proposed, the rates are illustrative, the balances are sample data. That's what the last page is for — a planning-stage case study should show its work, not a victory lap.

The carousel is the eight pages I'd walk through in a review.

Case study, marketing site, desktop app and mobile app are all live — link in the first comment.
