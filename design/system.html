Rules
  • Money uses tabular figures and two decimals, always: ₦268,400.00.
  • Amounts ≥ ₦100,000 keep grouping commas; the currency symbol never wraps.
  • Negative balances are shown with a minus and red, never brackets.
  • Serif italic is a garnish — one phrase per screen at most.
  • References are mono, uppercase, and copyable by tap or click.
  • Body copy sits at ≥ 4.5:1 contrast in both themes; muted labels at ≥ 4.5:1 on surfaces.
Spacing: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 22 · 30. Radii: 8 · 12 · 16 · 22 · 30 · pill. Tap targets stay ≥ 44 × 44 px.
